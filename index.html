    How to play

    1. Youngest explorer rolls first — then pass the device clockwise.
    2. Tap Roll Dice (or Space). Your champion hops square by square.
    3. Land on a gold ring to climb a sky-ladder. Land on a red ring and a serpent drags you down.
    4. Rolling a 6 grants a bonus roll — but three 6s in a row skips your move.
    5. First to square 100 takes the crown. Exact roll required (if enabled).
    6. Hover any tile to spotlight its serpent or ladder — the rest falls quiet. The 🐍 button up top fades (👻) or hides (🚫) all routes for a clean board.
    7. Shortcuts: Space roll · C camera · M mute · H help · Esc close.
