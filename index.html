    How to play

    1. The tides draw who rolls first — then pass the device around (or hand seats to 🤖 CPU sailors from the crew screen).
    2. Tap Roll Dice (or Space). Your champion hops square by square.
    3. Land on a gold ring to climb a sky-ladder. Land on a red ring and a serpent drags you down.
    4. Rolling a 6 grants a bonus roll — but three 6s in a row skips your move.
    5. Classic: first to square 100 takes the crown (exact roll if enabled). Swift: first past 50 wins.
    6. Interrupted? Your voyage auto-saves every turn — resume it anytime from the crew screen.
    7. Hover any tile to spotlight its serpent or ladder — the rest falls quiet. On touch screens, tap a tile to inspect it. The 🐍 button up top fades (👻) or hides (🚫) all routes for a clean board.
    8. 📺 Auto director (default) holds a top view, cuts to the dice, tracks every hop, slide and climb — then settles home. Grab the camera anytime with drag/scroll; it hands control straight back and resumes when you're idle.
    9. 🎨 Graphics auto-adapts to your device (Eco → Cinematic). Force a tier from the pause menu — your voyage is saved across the reload.
    10. Shortcuts: Space roll · C camera · M sound (all · music-off · mute) · H help · Esc pause.
